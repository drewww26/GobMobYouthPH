About Us

GOVMOB Youth (Governance and Mobility Youth Alliance) is a youth-led organization committed to empowering young people to become active participants in shaping their communities and society. We promote active citizenship, policy literacy, and meaningful engagement by equipping youth with the knowledge, skills, and platforms necessary to address pressing social issues. Through structured programs, advocacy campaigns, and partnerships, we foster collaboration and innovation among young leaders.

Explore Committees
📋

Committee on Secretariat

Responsible for the official documentation, record-keeping, and management of all organizational archives. They handle meeting minutes, resolutions, membership directories, and internal communications to ensure seamless administrative operations.

💰

Committee on Finance and Budget

Oversees the financial health of the organization. This includes budget planning, tracking expenditures, managing funds, financial reporting, and spearheading fundraising initiatives to sustain official projects and programs.

🔍

Committee on Audit

Ensures accountability and transparency within the alliance. They conduct regular reviews of financial statements, inspect assets, and evaluate operational procedures to ensure strict compliance with the Constitution and By-Laws.

📢

Committee on Public Information and Media

Manages the public image, press relations, and digital footprint of the organization. They handle social media content, website updates, graphic design, and photography to promote advocacy and engage the youth community.

👥

Committee on Membership and Recruitment

Leads the application, screening, and orientation process for prospective members. They are also responsible for monitoring member welfare, organizing team-building activities, and facilitating leadership development pathways.

⚖️

Committee on Ethics and Discipline

Upholds the moral standards and integrity of the organization. They review conflict resolution cases, investigate grievances, interpret constitutional rules, and ensure all members strictly adhere to the ethical code of conduct.

JOIN NOW

Membership in GOVMOB YOUTH is open to individuals within the youth sector who:

  • A Resident/Voter Youth of Muntinlupa aged 15-30 Years old
  • Membership is open to individuals within the youth sector who align with the vision, mission, and objectives.
  • Applicants must agree to uphold the GOVMOB Youth Constitution and By-Laws and comply with its rules and procedures.
  • Prospective members must demonstrate commitment to ethical conduct and active participation in organizational activities.
  • All applicants are subject to the prescribed application, screening, and orientation process.

Expectations for Members

  • Active Participation Attend assemblies, meetings, and committee work.
  • Ethical Standard Uphold transparency, accountability, and integrity.
  • Program Support Support organizational programs and official initiatives.
  • Rule Compliance Respect parliamentary procedures and internal rules.
  • Disciplinary Review Failure to meet expectations may lead to internal review.

Pathways for Members

  • Leadership Training Participate in leadership programs and institutional mentoring.
  • Policy and Advocacy Gain exposure to policy research and advocacy work.
  • Committee Leadership Take on committee leadership roles and officer candidacy.
  • Partnerships Engage in forums, community programs, and organizational partnerships.
  • Advancements Progress within the organization is based on performance and achievement.
