JOIN NOW

Membership in GOVMOB YOUTH is open to individuals within the youth sector who:

  • A Resident/Voter Youth of Muntinlupa aged 15-30 Years old
  • Membership is open to individuals within the youth sector who align with the vision, mission, and objectives.
  • Applicants must agree to uphold the GOVMOB Youth Constitution and By-Laws and comply with its rules and procedures.
  • Prospective members must demonstrate commitment to ethical conduct and active participation in organizational activities.
  • All applicants are subject to the prescribed application, screening, and orientation process.

Expectations for Members

  • Active Participation Attend assemblies, meetings, and committee work.
  • Ethical Standard Uphold transparency, accountability, and integrity.
  • Program Support Support organizational programs and official initiatives.
  • Rule Compliance Respect parliamentary procedures and internal rules.
  • Disciplinary Review Failure to meet expectations may lead to internal review.

Pathways for Members

  • Leadership Training Participate in leadership programs and institutional mentoring.
  • Policy and Advocacy Gain exposure to policy research and advocacy work.
  • Committee Leadership Take on committee leadership roles and officer candidacy.
  • Partnerships Engage in forums, community programs, and organizational partnerships.
  • Advancements Progress within the organization is based on performance and achievement.
